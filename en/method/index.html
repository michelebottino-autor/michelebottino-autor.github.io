Sources / Structure / Verification

Method

Alongside novels and essays, Michele Bottino develops methods for examining documents and information. The underlying idea is simple: before recounting an event, one must know its source and which questions remain open.

The method does not replace research or make uncertain information certain. It helps distinguish what a document proves, what someone reports and what remains a hypothesis.

Investigative writing course — ten units with interactive exercises (IT)

General approach

When sources are incomplete or conflicting, Bottino proposes comparing them without supplying missing passages. Only after gathering the data can a narrative be constructed and each claim checked for support.

The principles are to identify sources, distinguish facts from interpretations, explain doubts and correct conclusions when new documents emerge.

Architecture of the methodological work

The publications address different problems, from archival research to AI-assisted writing. Each explains the transition from collected material to conclusions.

Purpose of the method

Methodological work does not replace critical judgement, but supplies tools for making visible how information is selected, classified, transformed and represented.

Its value lies in distinguishing precisely what is documented, reported, inferred and what must remain identified as a limitation.

Acronyms and research scope

BM-OP identifies an operational component devoted to documentation and inference control; RCTM is the contextual reconstruction protocol from minimal documentary traces; IMPERIUM identifies a project component of the research system. These terms refer to different areas and must not be confused with external validation.

Each application must distinguish observable sources, transcriptions and data, interpretative hypotheses, replicable procedures and verified results. Performance claims, independent checks or comparative results will be stated only when supported by publicly checkable protocols, materials and evidence.
