How to get in touch

Use the official contact page to send a message. The form is hosted on the Italian-language website; correspondence in English and Italian is welcome.

For an archival or historical enquiry, include a concise description of the document or question, its known provenance, the reference or shelfmark if available, and the specific type of comparison requested. A short, source-linked proposal is more useful than a general request.
