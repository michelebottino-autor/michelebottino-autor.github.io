Historical research

The Archivio Documentale Calatabiano (ADC) studies historical evidence relating to eastern Sicily, its institutions and its Mediterranean connections.

Archive and scope

The ADC is an independent research initiative founded and coordinated by Michele Bottino. Its subject matter includes historical charters, feudal documentation, ecclesiastical institutions, fortifications, historical geography, visual materials and the transmission of documentary evidence.

The work distinguishes original records, published editions, digital reproductions and secondary scholarship. Each interpretation must remain traceable to its supporting evidence, while unresolved questions are recorded as such.

Calatabiano, March 1213

A royal act issued at Messina by Constance and Henry, concerning the castle of Calatabiano and the Church of Catania, survives in printed diplomatic editions. The existing ADC comparison distinguishes the royal confirmation from a purchase mentioned in the act.

The spelling Arnaldo appears in the printed source. Identifying this figure with Armaleo Monaldeschi requires separate justification. The medieval original has not been examined directly in this work.

Medieval and early modern contexts

Research also considers the institutional history of Calatabiano, fifteenth- and sixteenth-century documents, historic local infrastructure and the military events of 1677. Interpretations remain open to further archival verification.

Methods and research outputs

The research programme uses source comparison, bibliographic verification, metadata checks and explicit records of uncertainty. It includes methodological publications by the author, among them:
