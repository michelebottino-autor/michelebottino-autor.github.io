About the author

Michele Bottino is an Italian writer and essayist. His work encompasses novels, nonfiction and independent historical research. His writing favours a restrained style, clear language and a consistent narrative structure.

Alongside his literary activity, he coordinates the Archivio Documentale Calatabiano (ADC), a research initiative dedicated to locating, organising and critically examining historical evidence about Calatabiano and its wider Sicilian and Mediterranean context.

For the full biographical account, see the English biography. This international page is an overview, not a complete translation of the Italian website.

International academic community

Michele Bottino is an external member of the Institute of Medieval and Early Modern Studies (IMEMS) at Durham University in the United Kingdom.

He welcomes scholarly correspondence concerning documentary interpretation, manuscript identification and historical source comparison. External membership does not imply a university appointment or a formal institutional research partnership.

Research enquiries and contact

For proposals relating to historical source criticism, archival documentation, bibliographic corrections or the literary catalogue, use the website's contact page. Correspondence in English or Italian is welcome. Research contributions must identify sources and permissions where applicable.
