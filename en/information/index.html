Privacy notice

This notice explains how the personal data of visitors to Michele Bottino’s official website are processed.

Data controller

Michele Bottino is the data controller.

For communications concerning the protection of personal data, use the details in the contact section.

Types of data processed

The site may process the following categories of data:

  • Data you voluntarily provide by email or through the contact and ADC contribution forms, such as your name, email address, message, description and provenance of the material, attribution preferences and terms of use.
  • Technical browsing data strictly necessary for the operation and security of the site.
  • Data that may be processed by the technical services involved in hosting and forwarding messages, including attachments you choose to send using the site’s forms.
  • Usage data collected by Google Analytics 4 only if you consent to statistics.

Purposes of processing

Personal data are processed exclusively to:

  • Receive and handle requests submitted through the contact form.
  • Ensure the proper technical functioning of the site.
  • Protect the site and related services against misuse or unlawful use.
  • Compile visit statistics only with the visitor’s consent.

Legal basis for processing

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on:

  • Steps taken at the data subject’s request before entering into a contract, or the legitimate interest in responding to communications, depending on the nature of the request.
  • Consent to the activation of Google Analytics 4.
  • The controller’s legitimate interest in maintaining the site’s security, continuity and integrity.
  • Pre-contractual steps where the contact concerns a specific request from the user.

How data are processed

Data are processed electronically and over communications networks, with appropriate measures to ensure security, confidentiality and proper use of the information collected.

Technical services involved

The site is published via GitHub Pages. Messages sent through the contact page and any attachments are forwarded by the external service FormSubmit to the controller’s official email address. You may also write directly by email.

These services may process technical data or information you submit voluntarily to the extent strictly necessary for their operation.

Data retention

Messages may pass through FormSubmit and email services. FormSubmit states that it retains form information for up to 30 days and does not retain attachments for retrieval via its API. Data received in the mailbox are kept only as long as necessary to handle the request, subject to legal requirements or the need to protect the controller’s rights.

External services and embedded content

Optional statistics use Google Analytics 4, which is loaded only if you opt in. You can get in touch through email links or the FormSubmit form. If you use the form, files you select are also sent to the provider for delivery by email. Facebook content on the homepage is embedded only when you activate the relevant control. Your browser may then communicate technical data to Meta. Please do not include sensitive data or confidential information in email messages.

Providers may operate outside Italy. Consult their respective notices and terms for their roles, processing locations, safeguards for any transfers and service-specific retention periods. These matters require documentary verification. This notice does not attest that such verification is complete.

Data recipients

Within the purposes described above, data may be processed by technical providers involved in operating or hosting the site or forwarding messages.

Memories and testimonies for the Archivio Documentale Calatabiano

The Memorie e racconti di Calatabiano page currently lets you prepare an email proposing a personal account to the editors. Preparing or saving a draft in your browser neither sends it nor means the Archive has received it.

Separate infrastructure has been prepared using Cloudflare Workers, a private Cloudflare D1 database and Turnstile spam protection. Automatic transmission to that archive is not yet active. Activation requires further verification of security measures, processing conditions and retention periods.

Once direct submissions are enabled, their purpose will be to collect, assess and, where appropriate, preserve personal accounts for historical and documentary research. The narrative, title, period and place, any name given for attribution, optional contact details and publication preferences may be processed. Publication will require separate authorisation and editorial assessment. Private contact details will not be published.

For information, corrections or withdrawal requests, use the contact page. The following retention criteria have been prepared and await validation before launch: 180 days for accounts awaiting assessment, 365 days for approved but unpublished accounts, and 30 days after withdrawal or rejection. Published accounts are to undergo editorial review at least every 24 months. Technical anti-spam counters are intended for deletion 7 days after expiry. Automatic deletion of accounts is still disabled. The legal basis for each purpose, any retention needed to meet legal obligations and the providers’ terms must be checked and specified before the service is activated.

Rights of data subjects

Where applicable law provides, you have the right to:

  • Obtain information about the processing of your personal data.
  • Access the data.
  • Request correction or updating.
  • Request erasure.
  • Obtain restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing in the cases provided for by law.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Counters in the Archivio Documentale Calatabiano

The visit counter shows the total number of sessions recorded in the ADC. To prevent repeated increments during one session, the browser keeps a temporary indicator in the tab.

With statistics enabled, the live indicator counts connections from visible tabs throughout the Archive. It does not distinguish individuals or create profiles. The service does not store IP addresses, identifiers, pages visited or an attendance history. Cloudflare receives the technical network data needed to manage connections.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The site does not operate its own advertising tools. Google Analytics 4 is optional and loads only after you accept statistics. Your choice and its date are stored locally in your browser. You are asked again after 180 days. The external CounterAPI counter is currently disabled.

For details of cookies and technical browsing tools, see the cookie policy.

Updates

This notice may be updated at any time for legal, technical or organisational reasons.

Corrections and requests concerning content

For information about correcting historical content and exercising your rights, see the corrections and rights notice. The dedicated private contact channel is not yet active.

Website accessibility

This notice covers Michele Bottino’s website and the sections of the Archivio Documentale Calatabiano (ADC). The aim is to make the content accessible to as many people as possible, including people who use a keyboard or assistive technology.

Available features

The main pages examined have a main heading, alternative text for images and a link to skip directly to the content. Further manual checks across the site are needed to assess the quality and completeness of these features.

Checks carried out and their limits

Preliminary checks covered the code of the main pages. Automated tests covered selected navigation features and the ADC counter. These tests do not constitute a full assessment against WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

The site therefore makes no claim of full conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA at present. No external body has issued a certificate of conformance.

Completing the assessment requires manual checks and testing with assistive technology, including:

  • Full keyboard operation and visible keyboard focus.
  • Colour contrast, text resizing and layout on small screens.
  • Screen reader compatibility and the accuracy of alternative text.
  • Operation of forms, menus and interactive content.
  • Checks on PDF documents and multimedia content, where present.

Report a difficulty

If you encounter a barrier when accessing a page or document, use the contact page and include the address of the affected page and a description of the problem. Reports help identify which improvements to make first.

Standards and checking tools

The following links lead to specifications and validation tools. They do not certify conformance or imply that any of the organisations listed has approved or certified the site.

  • W3C WAI — WCAG guidelines — International reference for accessible web content.
  • W3C Nu Validator — Tool for checking HTML syntax.
  • W3C CSS Validation Service — Tool for checking style sheets.
  • AgID — Accessibility and usability — Information from the Italian public authority.

Successful HTML and CSS validation alone does not demonstrate WCAG conformance. The site does not display WCAG or AgID logos or GDPR badges as evidence of conformance.

References

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 set out the international technical criteria. Which legal obligations apply depends on the nature of the organisation or individual and the services offered.

Corrections and protection of personal data

If you find inaccurate information, an attribution that lacks sufficient documentation or material that affects your privacy, you may ask for it to be checked. Each report is assessed in light of the sources, individual rights and the public interest in historical knowledge.

Confidential contact: There is currently no private channel for submitting requests through this site. Do not post documents or personal data in comments or on social media. A dedicated contact channel still needs to be activated.

Corrections to publications on Calatabiano

Four notes were added to the Zenodo records on 26 November 2026. Before citing the passages concerned, consult the note associated with the DOI of the edition.

  • Calatabiano, Volume I — dating of the files, attribution of the investiture and quantification of the assets.
  • L’Egemonia dell’Oro Bianco — sugar production and relations with ecclesiastical institutions.
  • Il processo feudale come infrastruttura economica — attribution of the delivery formulas and the causal connection between the files.
  • Applicazione del protocollo RCTM a Calatabiano — the location of Santo Marchetto and the relationships between water, woodland, jurisdiction and productive activities.

What you can report

  • Errors in transcription, dating, identification or attribution of sources.
  • Unnecessary disclosure of personal data or identifiable images.
  • Requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection, where provided for by law.
  • Questions about rights to reproduce texts, photographs or documents.

Preparing a request

Once the confidential channel is active, identify the page, the passage to be checked and the correction requested. Send no more personal data than necessary.

Response times

Requests to exercise GDPR rights normally require a response within one month of receipt, even if refused. The deadline may be extended in the circumstances provided for by law, with notice to the person concerned.

How content is assessed

A request may lead to a correction, clarification of the sources, a reduction in the data published or, where necessary, removal of the content. For historical documents we distinguish the original, the transcription and the interpretation. Online publication is not always necessary for archival preservation.

Removing a Google search result is a separate matter from removing the document from this site. Any decision depends on the individual case. It does not entail automatic deletion of every historical reference.

Legal sources and references

The framework considered includes Articles 5, 17, 21 and 89 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Italian Privacy Code and the Italian data protection authority’s ethical rules for archiving in the public interest and historical research. For details about processing on the site, see theprivacy notice. The editorial procedure is no substitute for a legal assessment of the individual case.
