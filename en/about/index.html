Michele Bottino

A literary career grounded in narrative structure, realism and an interest in historical evidence.

Life and writing

Born in Catania, Sicily, in 1972, Michele Bottino is an Italian novelist, essayist and independent researcher. His fiction and nonfiction explore personal experience, the workings of power and the influence of historical circumstances on individual lives.

His approach to writing favours direct language, formal consistency and close attention to the circumstances that shape characters and events. His books range from psychological and investigative fiction to social commentary and historical writing.

His published works include Stige, Il deposito delle anime, Intrighi Invisibili, Oniro, EUROPA: Anatomia di un crimine and L’Uomo Consapevole. Original Italian book titles are used throughout this site.

Historical research

Alongside his literary activities, Bottino founded and coordinates the Archivio Documentale Calatabiano (ADC). This independent research project examines historical documents concerning Calatabiano, eastern Sicily and the broader Mediterranean world.

Its working method separates what a source explicitly records from later scholarly interpretations and from propositions that remain unverified. Digital collections, archival descriptions, printed editions and historical photographs are treated as distinct types of evidence.

Bottino has also developed documentary-analysis frameworks including the RCTM protocol and the Integrated Archival Analysis System, presented in his publicly linked research publications.

International research community

Bottino is an external member of the Institute of Medieval and Early Modern Studies (IMEMS), Durham University, United Kingdom.

He is also the founder and editor of the independent Archivio di Studi Storici e Documentali community on Zenodo, which presents historical and documentary research. Participation in open scholarly platforms does not itself constitute external peer review.

Bibliographic identifiers and related works can be checked through his ORCID profile.
