Read the structural analysis of Denise Pipitone (Italian)

The disappearance of Denise Pipitone is an established fact. Over the years, however, investigations and numerous reports have not led to a conclusive reconstruction of what happened to her.

Inquiries have followed different directions, including outside Italy. Some leads have been ruled out; others have not found sufficient corroboration. The material collected is extensive, but it does not form a sequence of facts capable of explaining the disappearance.

Reports and possible identifications have played a particular role. Each time, it has been necessary to distinguish what someone believed they had seen from what could be verified. The frequency of reports does not in itself constitute evidence.

Public attention has been rekindled several times, often accompanied by the expectation of a breakthrough. From an investigative standpoint, however, a new report does not necessarily amount to a decisive element. Media coverage and the results of inquiries follow different timelines.

The principal difficulty remains this: the leads examined have not made it possible to connect the available elements into a unified, verified reconstruction. It is the distance between the amount of information circulated and what can actually be demonstrated that leaves the case unresolved.
