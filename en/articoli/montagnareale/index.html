The Montagnareale case

What we really know

Classification: Real case / Documentary analysis

Case status: Open, with inquiries continuing at the time described

Level of certainty: Partial, with the sequence of events still uncertain

Method applied: Bottino System (MRN)

Tags: Montagnareale, triple homicide, hunters, crime reporting, documentary analysis, open case

Update: The investigation has moved beyond its initial phase. After the three bodies were found, inquiries focused on post-mortem examinations, ballistics, mobile phone cell-site records, a witness and, subsequently, a person under investigation whose firearms were seized. In mid-February 2026, partial admissions of having been present and having fired shots were also reported. These developments have yet to establish a definitive account of what happened.

This reconstruction covers the facts and developments known in the period under review. Theories about the sequence of events remain provisional until corroborated.

In the final weeks of January 2026, three men were found shot dead in woodland in the Caristia district of Montagnareale. Initial reports placed the deaths in the context of hunting in an isolated part of the Nebrodi mountains.

The initial inquiries concerned post-mortem examinations, ballistics, phone records and witness accounts. The main questions were who was present and in what order the shots were fired. At this stage the available evidence did not establish responsibility or motive with certainty.

As the investigation progressed, further details emerged. There was a witness, a person came under investigation, firearms were seized, and technical checks examined the consistency of the gunfire evidence and the reconstructed positions. Partial statements about being in the area and firing shots were also reported. These are important investigative developments, but they do not amount to a judicial finding.

It remains necessary to establish how the events unfolded and what weight the evidence carries when considered as a whole. A more specific line of inquiry does not in itself establish the sequence of events.

This article sets out the state of the investigation as described in the sources consulted. It does not ascribe a definitive outcome to it. Any later developments will need to be assessed against the available records and corroborating evidence.
